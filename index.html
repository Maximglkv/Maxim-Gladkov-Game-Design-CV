Solo Project · Atmospheric Horror

Soulin

Sole Creator · Design, Development, Art & Audio

Created entirely by me, Soulin is a slow-paced, retro-styled horror game about monitoring a house through security cameras as paranormal activity grows more aggressive each night. I handled the project end to end, from its design and gameplay to its rooms, assets, UI, audio, and atmosphere.

Solo Game Project

  • Designed a camera-monitoring and memory-based loop: observe rooms before the activity period, spot what has changed, and correct disturbed objects.
  • Structured progression across three days, gradually unlocking rooms and increasing the player's observation and memory demands.
  • Planned a final-night boss encounter that turns the object's repair mechanic into a faster, higher-pressure challenge.
  • Developed the game's retro visual direction, household assets, camera-switching audio, and atmospheric horror presentation.
